Madrid Campuses | Ie University

A campus in the heart of the capital

The city campuses put students next to multinational companies, start-ups and public institutions, a few minutes away from each other by public transport.

Classrooms, libraries and study areas are open every day of the week, and every building has spaces for group work and quiet study.

Life on campus

Student clubs, sports facilities and cultural events fill the calendar of the academic year, and the career services team organises meetings with recruiters every month.
