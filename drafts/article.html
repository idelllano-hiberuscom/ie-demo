The impact of logistics in cities

This report looks at how urban deliveries affect the street and proposes strategies, actions and policies to reduce that impact. It compares delivery scenarios built on consolidation centres, small neighbourhood hubs and parked vans, and weighs their efficiency against the space and noise they take from residents.
