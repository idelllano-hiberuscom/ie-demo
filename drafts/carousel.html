A LEED Gold building, built to keep up

Sustainability shaped the Tower from the brief stage. The building holds LEED Gold certification, but the more telling decisions are the ones that never show up in a logo.

Location

Direct access to public transport and the Madrid Nuevo Norte regeneration project means most of the building's daily population arrives without a car.

Energy

Building-wide climate and lighting control, with consumption monitored floor by floor.

Operations

IE University has set a net-zero emissions target for 2030 across all campuses, supported by a responsible-consumption framework introduced in 2020.

Space efficiency

A vertical campus concentrates more than 50,000 m² of academic, social and innovation space within a single tower, reducing land use and supporting a denser, lower-impact urban model.
