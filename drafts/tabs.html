Why study business management with us

Our programs are designed around the challenges companies face today: digital transformation, sustainability and the need for leaders who can work across cultures and disciplines.

Every program includes a capstone project with a real company, career coaching from the first term and access to the entrepreneurship centre, where students can test their own ventures.

Flexible formats

Choose between full-time, part-time, online and blended formats. Online students follow live sessions with the same faculty and take part in the same international weeks as their on-campus peers.

A global community

Classes bring together students from more than 100 nationalities, and the alumni network organises events, mentoring programs and job opportunities in every region of the world.

Admissions

The admissions process is the same for every format: an online application, an admissions test or a recognised standardised test, and a personal interview with a member of the admissions team.

Scholarships and financial aid are available for every program. Apply early to be considered for the widest range of options.
